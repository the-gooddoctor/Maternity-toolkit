# Partner / co-parent / surrogacy intended-parent / solo / same-sex modes (v2.3 pass 9 of 12)

## Core conceptual shift

Partner mode in v2.2 was a content filter. **v2.3 makes it a path** — a dedicated Home tab layout with feature-set re-weighting toward what the partner actually operates (contraction timer, bag list, appointment calendar, birth plan). Once a partner picks their mode, they get a Home built for them, to-dos written for them, education aimed at them.

## New SED-CC-* IDs integrated into v2.3

**SED-CC-004b** `pregnantPersonName` + `pregnantPersonPronouns` fields (local-only).

**SED-CC-004c** `partnerRole: 'co_parent' | 'birth_companion' | null` sub-selection. For same-sex couples: HFEA 2008 ss.42–43 framework. F08 to-do "Confirm both parents will be named on the birth certificate"; F07 "Both parents present, both recognised" prompt; Stonewall signposting for complex cases.

**SED-CC-004d** Partner-mode dashboard — visual marker ("PC" / "IP" header icon); one-tap mode switcher (for `shared_device`); cards reordered to "How [name] might be feeling this week" + "What you can do this week"; partner-specific tile row; "For partners" library entry.

**SED-CC-004e** Partner education content library — 9 sections covering pregnancy basics; preparing for birth (gas and air, epidural, caesarean explained); during labour (presence not fixing); first hours and days; first 6 weeks postnatal reality for partners; practical and legal (paternity leave, ShPP, second-parent birth registration); if things are hard (Birth Trauma Association partner; Bliss for NICU; PANDAS for partners); surrogacy IP track; solo-parent track. Gender-neutral throughout.

**SED-CC-004f** Surrogacy IP path — parental-order timeline (E/W in v1.0; Scotland v1.0.x); Brilliant Beginnings, Surrogacy UK, COTS, Cafcass signposting. F08 surrogacy track: parental order within 6 months of birth.

**SED-CC-004g** Solo-parent path — `soloParent` flag; "Your support network" panel replaces partner content; F07 "I will be on my own and that is my choice" option carries onto PDF; F08 solo-specific items.

**SED-CC-004h** Donor-conception opt-in (v1.0.x; Donor Conception Network signposting).

**SED-CC-004i** Long-distance partner mode (v1.0.x; labour-call plan, travel logistics, video attendance).

**SED-CC-004j** Older-children-in-household toggle (v1.0.x; sibling preparation, childcare plan for labour day).

## Things NOT to do (codified)

No "support your woman" framing; no assumption partner is male / heterosexual / cohabiting / biological / lives with pregnant person; no "10 things every dad should know" listicle copy; no infantilising content; no automatic partner notification of pregnant user's state change.
