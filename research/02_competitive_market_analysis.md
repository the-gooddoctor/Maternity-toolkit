## 2. Validated PRD claims

- **"Pregnancy+ at £3.99/month."** Directionally accurate.
- **"Flo at approximately £43/year."** Range is now £39.99–£49.99/year.
- **"Approximately 650,000 UK births per year."** Slightly out-of-date — 655,000–660,000 for UK 2024 (ONS 594,677 E&W + ~45,000 Scotland + 19,416 NI). First increase since 2021.
- **"Over 50% of pregnant women download at least one pregnancy app."** Supported by PMC systematic review and Oxford 2024 study.
- **ONS baby names dataset** is public, **OGL v3.0**, currently 2024 dataset.
- **Privacy violations by competitors** — strongly validated. Flo FTC settlement (2021/2023); $56M Flo+Google class action settled Sept 2025 over SDK data leakage; ICO 2023–2024 review of period/fertility apps found transparency gaps.
- **Insensitive pregnancy loss handling** — 2021 study of 166 apps: 72% don't account for loss at all; only ~10% link out.

---

## 3. Corrections

1. UK births figure: update to "approximately 655,000 UK births in 2024 (ONS, NRS Scotland, NISRA), the first increase in E&W since 2021."
2. Flo annual price: widen to ~£39.99–£49.99/year.
3. **Baby Buddy is now part of Babyzone** (March 2025).
4. "NHS Pregnancy app" is a misnomer — multiple regional apps (Badger Notes, Mum & Baby, etc.).
5. Tommy's "app" is a clinician web tool, not a consumer app.
6. App Store category: **Health & Fitness** (not Medical).

## 4. Competitive differentiation review

The PRD's positioning table holds up well. **One competitive risk under-weighted:** Baby Buddy (Babyzone) is the closest direct overlap with Seed's free tier. Seed's differentiation: the Pro toolkit and the offline/no-account architecture.

**Gap surfaced:** Pregnancy-after-loss apps are a distinct, growing category — Seed could note as v1.1 enhancement.

## 5. Pricing and revenue recommendations

**£4.99 price:** genuinely differentiated. No direct competitors offer one-time purchase in UK. Hold £4.99 for launch; A/B at month 4 between £3.99/£4.99/£6.99.

**Revenue projections:** median freemium conversion is ~2.18% — the PRD's 5% target is **2.3× the median**. Recommend reframing:

| Scenario | Free downloads | Conv. | Pro purchases | Net @ £4.16 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pessimistic | 3,000 | 2% | 60 | £250 |
| Realistic-conservative | 10,000 | 3% | 300 | £1,250 |
| Target | 15,000 | 5% | 750 | £3,120 |
| Optimistic | 30,000 | 5% | 1,500 | £6,240 |

**App Store small-business programme** (15% commission) requires opt-in.
