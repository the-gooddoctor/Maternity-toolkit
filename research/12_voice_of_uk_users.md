## Daily ritual map

- Morning glance: dashboard week + days + countdown + fact-of-day
- Mid-morning kick check (mid-pregnancy+): 1-tap to Movement diary
- Bedtime reflection: optional mood note + one-line journal
- Sunday-night planning: F08 to-dos + F09 next appointment
- Worry moment: SED-CC-030 universal "Call your maternity unit" footer
- Labour day: F03 Labour Mode full-screen takeover
