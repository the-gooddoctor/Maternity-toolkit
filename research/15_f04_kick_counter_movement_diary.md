# F04 kick counter → Movement diary (v2.3 pass 4 of 12)

## Critical content finding

**The cold-drink prompt that appeared in the deep-dive brief is incompatible with Tommy's Movements Matter campaign**, which exists specifically to combat that myth. Telling worried users to "try a cold drink first" delays clinical presentation. v2.3 codifies as **SED-F04-ANTI** and **SED-UX-027 AP-21**. This is the single most important regulatory + safety finding in pass 3.

## Two re-framings

1. **Rename from "Kick counter" to "Movement diary"** (SED-F04-014). "Counter" implies a number matters; "diary" implies writing down what you noticed. RCOG GTG-57 2nd ed deprecates count-based methodology.
2. **Signposting before counting, not above it** (SED-F04-015). The Tommy's "call now" guidance moves from page-furniture to the first screen of the journey.
