# F03 contraction timer + Labour Mode (v2.3 pass 3 of 12)

## v2.2 audit

**Strong:** regulatory positioning air-tight (SED-F03-007/014); timestamp-delta engineering (SED-ARCH-011); 120pt buttons + haptic + keep-screen-on; "Call your maternity unit" pre-stored; partner coaching block.

**Thin:** partner coaching is one static paragraph (no mode switch); session-end summary one-sentence; no Live Activity / lock-screen surface; no multi-session "this labour" grouping; no accidental-end safeguard; no battery surfacing; no driving-to-hospital state; no qualitative log; doula support not addressed.

## SED-F03-ANTI catalogue

No labour-stage language ("active labour", "established labour", "transition", "dilation"); no threshold-triggered prompts; no colour-coded urgency; no Friedman / population curves; no push notifications about labour data; no "go to hospital now" recommendation; no auto-detection of "this labour"; no on-device ML / LLM / AI summaries.
